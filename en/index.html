About us

PLUFF is a modern company specializing in the production of high-precision plastic molds and parts. It started operations in 2025. SIA PLUFF was founded by JSC “SAF Tehnika”, JSC “HansaMatrix”, the Latvian Electronics and Electrical Engineering Industry Association, LLC “Company NA”, and LLC “Erica Synths”.
